ABOUT OUR PRACTICE

Cobscook Dentistry is Dr. Eric Bokinsky, DDS and his wife Carol Bokinsky, CRNA. Dr. Bokinsky has over ten years of experience in private practice and hospital dentistry. He has also held Assistant Professorships at Virginia Commonwealth University directing the Advanced Education in General Dentistry residency and teaching post-doctoral residents at the University of Virginia.
